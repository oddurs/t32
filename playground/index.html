Playground

Type a date or a T32 stamp, and see the span of time it stands for.

Examples

You can type

A date
2026-10-31
A date and time, in UTC
2026-10-31 15:30
Your local time
Add your offset: 2026-10-31 11:30-04:00
A T32 stamp
G0S7G6B1
The time right now
now

In the box, ↑ and ↓ step to the next and previous stamp. Everything runs in your browser. The address keeps your input, so a link shows the same thing.

G0S7G6B1

G0S7G6B1 stands for one second, on 31 October 2026, from 03:13:00 to 03:13:01 UTC.

How precise

Each character is 32 times finer than the one before it, so a shorter stamp names a longer span.

How to read it

Read a stamp from the left. Each character picks one of 32 equal parts of the span before it; the bar shows which.

  1. G 31 Dec 1999, 23:59:28 to 25 Oct 3088, 03:45:31
  2. G0 31 Dec 1999, 23:59:28 to 9 Jan 2034, 13:36:27
  3. G0S 1 Aug 2026, 00:52:43 to 24 Aug 2027, 09:33:15
  4. G0S7 24 Oct 2026, 23:46:35 to 6 Nov 2026, 03:02:51
  5. G0S7G 31 Oct 2026, 01:24:43 to 31 Oct 2026, 10:30:51
  6. G0S7G6 31 Oct 2026, 03:07:07 to 31 Oct 2026, 03:24:11
  7. G0S7G6B 31 Oct 2026, 03:12:59 to 31 Oct 2026, 03:13:31
  8. G0S7G6B1 31 Oct 2026, 03:13:00 to 31 Oct 2026, 03:13:01
