# Getting help

| You want to | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Ask how something works, or how to use the crate or `t32.ts` | [Discussions, Q&A](https://github.com/oddurs/t32/discussions/categories/q-a) |
| Propose a change to the specification, or weigh in on an open question | [Discussions, Ideas](https://github.com/oddurs/t32/discussions/categories/ideas) |
| Report text that is wrong or can be read two ways, a bug, or an implementation | [Issues](https://github.com/oddurs/t32/issues/new/choose) |
| Report a vulnerability | Privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes |

The [guide](https://oddurs.github.io/t32/docs/) answers the common questions, and the [playground](https://oddurs.github.io/t32/playground/) shows what any stamp or date means. Answers come from people who give their time; a small example that shows the question gets a faster one.
