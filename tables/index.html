9TAI − UTC, 1961–1971, and its steps

Before 1972, UTC ran at an adjusted rate. TAI − UTC = a + (MJD − m) × r seconds, where MJD is the Modified Julian Date of the UTC instant. Before 1961 it is defined as 0.
