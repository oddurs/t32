# Changelog

Changes to the specification are recorded in Appendix D of [SPEC.md](SPEC.md). This file records releases of the `t32` crate and of the repository's other artifacts.

## t32 0.3.0 (crate) and specification draft 0.3, 2026-11-01

The crate's minor version follows the draft it implements.

- **Specification:** draft 0.3, errata from a review of draft 0.2; Appendix D lists every change. Status section, open questions (Appendix C) and change history (Appendix D). The 1966 vector uses length 14, following the resolution rule for a nanosecond source.
- **Vectors:** `vectors/t32-vectors.json`, 782 conformance vectors, every one generated by the oracle, errors included. New categories cover TAI-instant encoding, binary packing, range bounds, the resolution rule, stamp plus duration, stamp minus stamp and provisional status.
- **Crate, fixed:**
  - `Stamp::max_len` is exact; it used floating point and could allow one character more than the specification permits near a midpoint between lengths.
  - `Duration::as_nanos` rounds to the nearest nanosecond, so every nanosecond count survives a round trip and the difference of instants made from nanoseconds is exact; it truncated twice and read 1 ns short.
  - Converting a negative `Duration` to `std::time::Duration` fails, however small; converting a long one no longer overflows.
  - `SystemTime` conversion works across the whole range in both directions.
  - `Sum` fails only when the total is out of range, not a partial sum.
  - UTC text is checked in the specification's order of errors, and a `.` among a duration's digits is `invalid_char`.
  - UTC readings follow the reading rule exactly at the edges of inserted time.
- **Crate, added:** `Stamp::upper_bound`, the range-query bound that is correct for mixed lengths (`Stamp::next` is not); `Instant::is_provisional`, so every conversion path reports provisional status.
- **TypeScript:** error codes are the specification's; parsing follows its grammar and order of errors; the resolution rule is exact; arguments are checked rather than wrapped; `upperBound`, `maxLength` and `fxIsProvisional` added.
- **Project:** governance, contribution guide, code of conduct, security policy and a list of implementations. CI gates every deploy and release.

## t32 0.2.0 (crate), 2026-10-31

First release on crates.io.

- **Types:** `Instant`, `Stamp`, `Duration`, `UtcDateTime` and `Error`, with exact integer arithmetic throughout.
- **Text and conversions:** RFC 3339 parsing and printing. Conversions to and from Unix time, TAI and `SystemTime`.
- **Spec support:** the full TAI − UTC history from 1961, and the specification's resolution rule as `Stamp::max_len`.
- **Serde:** optional serde support.
- **Compatibility:** Rust 1.70 or later.
