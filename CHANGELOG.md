# Changelog

Changes to the specification are recorded in Appendix D of [SPEC.md](SPEC.md). This file records releases of the `t32` crate and of the repository's other artifacts.

## Unreleased

- **Specification:** status section, open questions (Appendix C) and change history (Appendix D). The 1966 vector now uses length 14, following the resolution rule for a nanosecond source.
- **Vectors:** `vectors/t32-vectors.json`, about 600 conformance vectors generated by the oracle, covering both sides of every boundary in Appendix A. Both implementations run them.
- **Crate:** UTC readings follow the specification's reading rule exactly at the edges of inserted time: an instant whose 2⁻⁴⁰ s unit straddles the edge of a pre-1972 insertion is now read by the instant itself, not by the end of its unit.
- **Project:** governance, contribution guide, code of conduct, security policy and a list of implementations.

## t32 0.2.0 (crate), 2026-10-31

First release on crates.io.

- **Types:** `Instant`, `Stamp`, `Duration`, `UtcDateTime` and `Error`, with exact integer arithmetic throughout.
- **Text and conversions:** RFC 3339 parsing and printing. Conversions to and from Unix time, TAI and `SystemTime`.
- **Spec support:** the full TAI − UTC history from 1961, and the specification's resolution rule as `Stamp::max_len`.
- **Serde:** optional serde support.
- **Compatibility:** Rust 1.70 or later.
